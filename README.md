# Did AI Make You Money This Month?

A Monday report your AI builds from your own exports: hours saved, leads, sales by source, and what changed. One page you can trust.

## The promise

When you finish, you'll have one number you trust, every week: your AI net. And you'll see what's behind it.

## Who it's for

- Coaches, creators and agencies who've started using AI in their business.
- Anyone paying for AI tools who can't say yet if the setup is paying off.

You don't need a dashboard. You need 2 exports and 25 minutes.

## What's inside

- `START_HERE.md`: the instructions your AI reads first. It builds the report with you, one question at a time.
- `templates/export-checklist.md`: which files to pull, which columns to keep, and what to delete before sharing.
- `templates/hours-log.md`: a short log of the jobs AI does for you and the hours each one saves.
- `templates/monday-report.md`: the one-page report layout.

## How to use it

1. Download this pack and unzip it.
2. Fill in `templates/hours-log.md`. It takes about 5 minutes.
3. Pull last week's sales and leads exports. Follow `templates/export-checklist.md`.
4. Open a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT. Add the pack folder and your 2 exports.
5. Type: `Read START_HERE.md and follow it.`
6. Save the report with the date in its name. Do it again next Monday.

## What it doesn't do

- It doesn't connect to your tools. You bring the exports.
- It doesn't run by itself each Monday. You start it.
- It can't guess a sale's source if your checkout didn't record one. It'll say "not counted".
- It tells you what happened. It doesn't fix your offers or your pages.
